From the Mississippi bluffs to the Red River.

Rachel's mother's family, the Neltons, Rivers and Canfields, were carpenters, contractors and insurance men in the river towns around Winona, Minnesota, with roots in Germany and Luxembourg. Her father's family, the Johnsons, Tysons, Bells and Williamses, farmed, ranched and soldiered in Choctaw County, Oklahoma, and the cotton country of north Texas.

Everything here comes from records: obituaries, censuses, draft cards, death certificates and the newspapers of the day.

About this site

This family history follows the records. Where Rachel's Ancestry tree disagrees with a record, the site shows what the record says and lists the change below.

proven an original record names it. probable a good secondary source that agrees with the records. lead a family claim or copied-tree link no record has confirmed. People who may be living are shown by name only. This is a public website; search engines are asked not to index it.
