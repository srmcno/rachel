From the Mississippi bluffs to the Red River.

Rachel's mother's family, the Neltons, Rivers and Canfields, were carpenters, farmers and insurance men in the river towns around Winona, Minnesota. Her father's family, the Johnsons, Tysons, Bells and Williamses, farmed and soldiered in Choctaw County, Oklahoma, and the cotton country of north Texas.
